Tips for great before & after posts

  • Match the two shots. Same angle, distance and lighting make the change easy to see.
  • Frame first, blur second. Use Zoom and Move picture to frame each panel, then draw the blur box. Changing the zoom afterwards moves the picture under the box.
  • Make the box a little bigger than the face. For videos, tap Blur face, box the first frame, then watch the whole export to check the blur follows.
  • Several photos? Add them all to one panel. Use Next photo to blur each one separately, and skip photos with no face.
  • Clean backgrounds. Plain walls work best for Replace with color or Blurred background. Tap Pick backdrop, then tap the wall.
  • Your logo. A PNG with a transparent background looks best. If your logo sits on a plain background, choose Remove plain background.
  • Exporting. Keep the tab open and the screen on until it finishes. Clips of 5 to 30 seconds work best.
  • Start with a template. Pick one and tap Apply template, then add your media. Save your logo and colors as a brand kit so it takes seconds next time.
  • Close with a call to action. Turn on the closing card and add your phone number or handle.
  • Posting from a phone. After export tap Share / save to phone, save to Photos, then post from Instagram.

Privacy and consent

Your photos and videos are processed on your device and are never uploaded. Your logo is saved only in this browser.

Always get the person's written consent before posting before-and-after content, even with the face blurred. Tattoos, scars, jewellery and distinctive features can still identify someone.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, Reveal is free while we launch. Exports carry a small "Reveal by ConfHub" mark. To remove it, join the Pro waitlist and we will email you when Pro is ready.

Why did my export not play on Instagram?

Use Chrome or Safari so you get an MP4, and keep clips under 90 seconds.

Will the blur follow moving faces?

It follows steady movement. If the person turns away or moves quickly the box can slip, so always watch the finished clip.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, on current Chrome and Safari. A computer is faster for long videos.

Can I add music?

Yes. Pick a style in the Background music card, or upload your own track. Our styles are generated in your browser, so they carry no licence fees. Popular songs can be muted or blocked on business accounts, so only use music you have the right to use.
